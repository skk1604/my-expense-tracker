Backup and restore

Export regularly to your iPhone Files app or iCloud Drive. JSON restores everything; CSV opens in Excel.

Important: Data is stored in this browser's local storage, not as a permanent file in Files. Keep backups. Clearing Safari website data, changing the app address, or using private browsing can remove access to data. Use a consistent HTTPS address for best reliability.

All amounts stay in their original currency. No automatic exchange rates. Alert imports require your approval; no unattended Gmail or notification access.
